=== left recursion groups to the left
start: expr
?expr: expr "-" N -> sub | N
N: [0-9]
--- "1-2-3"
(start (sub (sub N"1" ~"-" N"2") ~"-" N"3"))
--- "1"
(start N"1")

=== indirect left recursion grows through both rules
start: l
?l: p "." X -> member | X
?p: p "(" N ")" -> call | l
X: "x"
N: "n"
--- "x(n)(n).x(n).x"
(start (member (call (member (call (call X"x" ~"(" N"n" ~")") ~"(" N"n" ~")") ~"." X"x") ~"(" N"n" ~")") ~"." X"x"))

=== a rule that is left and right recursive at once groups to the right
start: e
?e: e "-" e -> sub | N
N: [0-9]
--- "1-2-3"
(start (sub N"1" ~"-" (sub N"2" ~"-" N"3")))

=== a precedence block parses by level and adds nodes only for aliases
start: expr
expr: %precedence {
    sum: left=sum op=("+" | "-") right=product -> binary
    product: left=product op=("*" | "/") right=unary -> binary
    unary: op="-" operand=unary -> neg
    power: base=atom "**" exponent=unary -> power
    atom: N | "(" expr ")"
}
N: [0-9]
%ignore " "+
--- "1 + 2 * 3"
(start (binary left=N"1" op="+" right=(binary left=N"2" op="*" right=N"3")))
--- "1 - 2 - 3"
(start (binary left=(binary left=N"1" op="-" right=N"2") op="-" right=N"3"))
--- "2 ** -1"
(start (power base=N"2" ~"**" exponent=(neg op="-" operand=N"1")))
--- "-2 ** 2"
(start (neg op="-" operand=(power base=N"2" ~"**" exponent=N"2")))
--- "2 ** 3 ** 2"
(start (power base=N"2" ~"**" exponent=(power base=N"3" ~"**" exponent=N"2")))
--- "(1 + 2) * 3"
(start (binary left=~"(" left=(binary left=N"1" op="+" right=N"2") left=~")" op="*" right=N"3"))

=== precedence levels are local names
start: expr
expr: %precedence {
    sum: left=sum "+" right=atom -> add
    atom: N
}
N: [0-9]
--- "1+2"
(start (add left=N"1" ~"+" right=N"2"))

=== a rule matched again while growing sees the entries made since
start: r1*
r1: (start+ | "b" "a") ^ "oops"
--- "ba"
error at 2: oops
